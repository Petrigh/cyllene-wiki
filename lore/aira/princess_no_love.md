# The Princess That Couldn't Love

Un cuento de reino lejano, con chiste final.

> There was, the legend says
> On a far away kingdom
> A beautiful princess
> In line for the throne
>
> She spent her days
> Out in the garden
> Building bouquets
> Along with her maiden
>
> At noon, every day
> The halls filled with suitors
> Trying to get their ways
> They had all heard the rumours
>
> The princess could not love
> No one was a good fit
> She waved her glove
> Not interested in the bit
>
> Either too young or too old
> Too smart, too dumb
> Either too calm or too bold
> Too excited, too numb
>
> Either too quiet or too loud
> Too large, too small
> Either too shy or too proud
> Too short, too tall
>
> There seemed to be no cure
> For her strange affliction
> But despite fate being unsure
> She didn't lose her conviction
>
> The solution was quite simple
> In fact you might laugh
> It was hiding behind a wimple
> Working as the castle's staff
>
> The princess realised with a sigh
> Her suitors were all men
> And that is the reason why
> She married her maiden.
{: .verse}
