# Marrow Quillane

*Example page — delete once you have real NPCs.*

## One breath

Slow, kind, and enormously tired; wants the party to leave the crypt alone
without having to explain why.

## Quick facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Ancestry / kind | Human, sixties |
| Does what | Undertaker and gravedigger |

| Faction | None, though [the Compact](../factions/the-ashen-compact.md) pays him |
| Status | Alive |
| Feeling about the party | Wary — they asked the one question he cannot answer |

## First impression

- **You notice** — lime dust worked permanently into his knuckles.
- **They sound like** — soft, unhurried, finishes other people's sentences.
- **They keep doing** — wipes his hands on his apron before he lies.

## Wants, fears, won't

- **Wants:** to die in Greyfen, in a marked grave, with someone to dig it.
- **Fears:** being made to open the sealed crypt himself.
- **Won't:** take money from the party. He is very clear about this.

## What the party knows

- *Session 1* — Told the party the crypt has been sealed forty years, by order
  of a magistrate who is now dead.
- *Session 1* — Knew the vintner's daughter by name, and did not ask whether
  she had been found.

## Connections

- Paid a retainer by [the Ashen Compact](../factions/the-ashen-compact.md), which he has not mentioned.
- Buried the last four magistrates of [Greyfen](../locations/greyfen.md).

## Heard at the table

> "Forty years is not so long. I have shoes older than that."

## If it comes to a fight

**Commoner** (MM p. 345), with Insight +4 and a shovel (1d6 bludgeoning).
He will not fight. He will walk away and lock a door behind him.
