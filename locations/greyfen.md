# Greyfen

*Example page — delete once you have real locations.*

## In a line

The last safe bed before the marsh, and the reason the marsh is not safe.

## Arriving

- **See:** roofs pitched too steep for the weather, and a chapel yard with more
  fresh soil than a town this size should need.
- **Hear:** pumps. Always pumps, somewhere under the streets.
- **Smell:** wet lime, peat smoke, and standing water.

## Who runs it

| | |
|---|---|
| Authority | Magistrate Ovine Tarr, appointed, absent most days |
| Real power | Whoever keeps the pumps turning |
| Population | ~2,000 |
| Wealth | Poor but not desperate; salvage money passes through |
| Attitude to strangers | Polite, unhelpful |

## Getting there

Two days east of Ess by the marsh road, longer after rain. The road is raised;
leaving it is how people go missing.

## Complications

- The pumps need a decision from the magistrate, who cannot be found.
- Any party seen at the crypt loses standing with the Compact.
