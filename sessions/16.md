# Sesion 16

Hacen trámites. Visitan primero la [muni](../locations/buildings/municipalidad_brolwund.md) y les piden que llenen un monton de papeles. Les cuentan del [consejo](../factions/consejo_brolwund.md). Van a ver a los [paladines](../factions/paladines_carnahan.md). They meet [Harold Greenhill](../characters/NPCs/harold_greenhill.md). [Valentine](../characters/NPCs/valentine.md) got raided by the paladins. They go to the [Night Market](../locations/buildings/night_market.md). Y encuentran la caja en el camion de los paladines. Back at Valentines, they open the box, at the bottom there's a [One Eyed Kraken](../factions/one_eyed_kraken.md). And two bottles of [RCaine](../items/rcaine.md), a red one and a yellow one.
