# Lore template

Copy into `lore/`. Use it for gods, kingdoms, wars, calendars, languages —
anything the world would know without the party being there.

---

```yaml
---
title: The Drowning of Ess
parent: Lore
summary: The year the lake was drained, and what the crown did to keep it drained
era: 431 years ago
---
```

## In brief

Two or three sentences. This is the version a well-read character could recite.

## How the party learned it

A book, a bard, an inscription — or common knowledge, in which case say so.

## Details

The parts that could matter mechanically or dramatically. Keep it shorter than
you want to.

## Disputed

Where sources disagree. Useful: it lets you retcon without lying.
