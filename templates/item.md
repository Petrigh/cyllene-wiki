# Item template

Copy into `items/`. Use it for magic items and for mundane objects with plot
attached — a ledger, a key, a sealed letter.

---

```yaml
---
title: The Sallow Lantern
parent: Items
summary: Burns without fuel, and shows what the fen swallowed
rarity: Uncommon
attunement: true
holder: Vess
---
```

## What it looks like

Describable before it is identified. Weight, material, wear, smell.

## What it does

Plain rules text. If it is homebrew, say so, and note the item it is balanced against.

- **Attunement:** required / not required
- **Charges:** how many, and how they come back

## What the party knows about it

- *Session 5* — Found in …; nobody has identified it yet.

## Where it came from

History as the party could learn it, from a sage, a library, or the thing itself.

<div class="spoiler" markdown="1">
**DM only — do not publish.** The curse, the tracker, or the person who wants
it back.
</div>
