# Sesion 12

Party rest after leaving [Elmer's](../locations/buildings/emporio_elmer.md). [Luthor](../characters/party/luthor.md), [Iri](../characters/party/iri.md) and [First](../characters/party/first.md) had [nightmares](../lore/pesadillas.md). [Goose](../bestiary/goose.md) nightmares.
They casted Detect magic and saw -> magia de Encantamiento. First and Iri went after some flowers. Iri got bald and First's feet were turned the other way. Then the whole party woke up. They followed a wisp. Casted Sleep on some [pixies](../bestiary/pixies.md). Got back to normal. Finished their rest.
Next morning they woke up to some offerings. Then a gnome, [Nox](../characters/NPCs/nox.md), showed up. Told them he and some others had a proposal. They followed into the [Jester's Court](../locations/buildings/templo_jester.md). Recovered some items for them after fighting two [Bee-Holders](../bestiary/bee_holders.md). Nox and [Axl](../characters/NPCs/axl.md) started to lead them to the [Jester's temple](../locations/buildings/templo_jester.md). Had nightmares on the first night of rest: First, Iri and [Mary](../characters/party/mary.md).
