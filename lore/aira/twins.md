# The Twins

Dos hermanos aventureros, una pícara y un mago, que terminan siendo estrellas.

> Our story begins in a small town
> The name of which no bells will ring
> Home to heroes now renown
> The famous half elven twins
>
> These were two siblings
> Born as parts of a whole
> Two sides on each as a shilling
> Themselves two halves of a soul
>
> Went on many an adventure
> And though they often disagreed
> When in battle, with a simple gesture
> They performed the Baker guarantee
>
> But as many epic tales
> Loss and sadness arise
> With the flick of an evil spell
> Our rogue meets her demise
>
> Between stolen goodbyes
> For he was no healer
> He honours her in the skies
> Must only look up to see her
>
> And many years after
> When his light finally flickered
> He once more heard her laughter
> Together again the rogue and the wizard
>
> Now they shine together
> On the path of new adventurers
> Above all that ever hurt them
> Tyrants, crooks and emperors
{: .verse}
