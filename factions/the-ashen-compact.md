# The Ashen Compact

*Example page — delete once you have real factions.*

## Standing with the party

**Wary.** Rises when the party brings them salvage they could have sold
elsewhere. Falls every time they are seen at the sealed crypt.

| Level | What it gets you |
|---|---|
| Hostile | Doors close, prices double, guides refuse the party |
| Wary | Business only, cash first |
| Trusted | Credit, a guide, and straight answers |
| Sworn | They will spend people on the party's problem |

## What they want

Everything the drained lake left behind, before anyone maps it properly.

## How they get it

Licences, retainers, and a standing arrangement with whoever signs permits.
They rarely need violence, which is exactly why they can afford it.

## What they have

- **People:** forty salvagers, a dozen who will fight
- **Money:** the largest cash reserve in the Sallowmarch
- **Reach:** every permit office between here and Ess
- **Weakness:** their claim is only as good as the magistrate who signed it
