# The Sallow Lantern

*Example page — delete once you have real items.*

## What it looks like

Brass, green with age, heavier than it should be. The glass is not glass. It is
warm even when unlit.

## What it does

While lit, it sheds 20 feet of dim light in which objects submerged or buried
within that radius appear as faint outlines, as though seen through water.
It does not reveal creatures, and it does not reveal anything placed there
deliberately by a living hand.

- **Attunement:** not required
- **Charges:** none; it lights and douses on command, once per turn

## What the party knows about it

Nothing. It has not been found yet.

## Where it came from

Salvage crews report one lantern per generation coming out of the fen. The
Compact buys them and does not resell them.
