# The Fierceless Pirate Queen

Un relato de marinero, contado por el único sobreviviente de la tripulación que la abordó.

> Get ye lads and lassies, all hands on deck
> to hear the story of the Fierceless Pirate Queen
> and what was born of that futile wreck
> the most horrifying thing I've ever seen
>
> Only sixteen, I was working as a sailor
> under the command of mean Captain Jack
> On his dark and dirty whaler
> a dreaded vision of rotten wood and sails black
>
> One day we saw them speed by
> The pirate queen and her all female crew
> Blood red sails hoisted high
> Ripping the blue seas through
>
> And as soon as he saw, whisky-and-danger
> Captain Jack said to us lads
> You, my men, will help me take her
> And that's one of my commands
>
> So we boarded her maidenship that very night
> And she drowned fighting with her lassies
> What came after was a gruesome sight
> A transformation in the ocean dark and ashy
>
> From all around us, every maiden
> Who gave her life in fight
> As something new rose and waken
> Sirens were born that night
>
> Claws of coral, scales of silver
> Dragged men down to their demises
> Stealing the black heart that killed her
> The Queen with her blood red tail rises
>
> They left me alive to tell the tale
> Every detail I remember
> From their darkened eyes to every scale
> Every evil man they dismembered
>
> They went on to plague the seas
> Singing spirits off the coast
> Salt like hair loose on the breeze
> Pirate girls but never ghasts
{: .verse}
