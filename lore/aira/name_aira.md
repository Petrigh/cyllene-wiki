# The Name of Aira

La balada fundacional del [reino](../../locations/settlements/aira.md).

> This is the sad story
> Of our lady, [Aira](../../characters/NPCs/aira.md) the Pure
> Forgive me if I can't find the rhymes
> At all times
> For it's so heartbreaking at its core
>
> They said she was an angel
> And this was no word of mouth
> Her golden beauty incandescent
> Iridescent
> Truly the jewel of the south
>
> Aira was the glowing sun
> On [Iranon's](../../characters/NPCs/iranon.md) eternal day
> But something was lurking
> Urging
> Coming to dark it away
>
> Every day was paradise
> For this royal family
> King, Queen and darling boy
> Soon destroyed
> By a brewing calamity
>
> A dark devil heard tale
> Of Aira's heavenly beauty
> [Enoch](../../characters/NPCs/enoch.md)  was his cursed name
> He came
> And tried to steal her cruelly
>
> But he became enraged
> When he saw she wouldn't leave
> Murdered her in cold blood
> And her beloved,
> The child that she conceived
>
> Took her body
> While Iranon gave chase
> And cowardly flew away
> A bird of prey
> Who left no trace
>
> The King fell to his knees
> Cried his heart out at the square
> Cursed at the skies
> Tears filled his eyes
> I saw it happen, I was there
>
> Now there lies her monument
> In her beloved memory
> Marble and gold
> For all to behold
> Beautifully sad sight to see
>
> But that wasn't enough
> So Iranon, in his wisdom
> Changed our name
> And we became
> Aira, the kingdom
{: .verse}
