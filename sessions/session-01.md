# Session 1 — Forty Years of Nothing

*Example log — delete once you have real sessions.*

## Previously

First session. The party met on the marsh road east of Ess, all four heading
to Greyfen for unrelated reasons, none of them good.

## What happened

- Arrived in Greyfen at dusk; the pumps were audible from a mile out.
- The Sallow Vine had one room and a vintner who had stopped pretending to work.
- Halle asked them to find her daughter Wren, missing eleven days.
- Marrow Quillane, in the chapel yard, knew Wren's name and did not ask whether
  she had been found.
- Vess noticed lime dust on the sealed crypt's chain — recently handled.
- The chain was cut, not broken. Someone with a key had wanted it to look forced.
- Factor Iselm of the Ashen Compact offered them a licence to enter, for a cut.
- The party declined, which Iselm wrote down.

## Decisions the party made

- Turned down the Compact's licence, so anything they take out of the crypt is
  legally the Compact's.
- Did not tell Halle about the cut chain.
