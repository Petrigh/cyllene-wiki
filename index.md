# The Ashen Compact

A running record of the campaign: who the party has met, where they have been,
what they owe, and what is hunting them.

Missed a game? Read the newest entry in [Sessions](sessions/).

## Where things are

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| [Characters](characters/) | The party, plus every named NPC they have actually met |
| [Locations](locations/) | Settlements, dungeons, regions, single memorable rooms |
| [Factions](factions/) | Groups with goals of their own, and where the party stands with them |
| [Sessions](sessions/) | One page per game night, newest first |
| [Quests](quests/) | Open jobs, debts, and promises — with who is waiting on them |
| [Bestiary](bestiary/) | Creatures the party has fought or studied |

## The short version of the story so far

Three sentences, rewritten after every session, so a returning player can
catch up in ten seconds. Replace this paragraph with yours.

## Table rules of thumb

- If it happened at the table, it is canon. If it is only in the DM's notes, it is not.
- Anyone may edit anything. History is in git, so nothing is ever really lost.
- Player-facing pages record **what the party knows**, not what is true.
