# Session log template

Copy into `sessions/`, name it `session-07.md`, and write it the same night
while you still remember the jokes.

---

```yaml
---
title: "Session 7 — The Empty Crypt"
parent: Sessions
nav_order: 7
date: 2026-09-05
summary: The party opened the sealed crypt and found forty years of nothing
players: [Ana, Jordi, Marta, Pau]
absent: [Nil]
---
```

## What happened

Beats, not a transcript. Aim for eight to twelve bullets — enough that the
night is reconstructable, few enough that writing it stays fun.
